## B. The "octaves" — wave propagation per scale

Each scale has its own natural wave band, separated from the next by many orders
of magnitude — this is the physically-real content of "specific octaves of
physics":

- **QCD**: gluon fields; pions as pseudo-Goldstone modes.
- **Nuclear**: giant dipole/quadrupole resonances (MeV γ).
- **Atomic**: X-ray → optical → UV transitions (eV–keV).
- **Molecular**: vibrational (IR, ~1e13–1e14 Hz), rotational (microwave).
- **Condensed**: phonons (acoustic), magnons, plasmons.
- **Human-sense window**: sound 20 Hz–20 kHz; light 400–700 nm (4.3–7.5e14 Hz).
- **Stellar**: helio-/asteroseismology p-modes and g-modes.
- **Cosmological**: baryon acoustic oscillations (BAO), CMB acoustic peaks.

## C. What actually makes a scale "stable" (real mechanisms)

1. **Mass gap** — bound state below decay threshold (QCD → proton).
2. **Potential barrier** — nuclear / Coulomb / chemical barriers.
3. **Conservation + superselection** — baryon number protects the proton.
4. **Symmetry protection** — topological insulators, skyrmions, defects.
5. **Degeneracy pressure** — white dwarfs (electrons), neutron stars (neutrons).
6. **Hydrostatic equilibrium** — stars balance gravity vs. radiation/gas pressure.
7. **Dynamical equilibrium** — galaxies via virialized dark-matter halos.

These seven are the grounded version of "geometric resonance stabilizes": the
stabilization is real, but the *mechanism differs per scale*. A cross-scale claim
must specify which mechanism is in play at each rung.
